# Design context

Audience: SlingMods.com visitors and Slingshot enthusiasts, including casual players on desktop and phones.
Use: a genuinely entertaining racing game for the company website; regional tracks, a small story campaign, winnings spent at a SlingMods garage.
Tone: user explicitly requests a dope, creative, fun, high quality game. Direction: SlingMods red, black, graphite and white; oversized condensed motorsport typography; reflective automotive materials and detailed coastal urban scenery. Prioritize recognizable Slingshot proportions and vehicle details, correct controls, responsive layouts and real gameplay. Brand is sponsor and upgrade destination. Reference-built vehicle is original artwork, not manufacturer CAD.
