After Dark v0.3.0 asset additions (September 9, 2026)
============================================================
Six product photos are hosted locally from SlingMods product pages for this owner-requested company game. They remain the rights of their respective owners. DDMWorks, Thermal R&D, and TricLED product names identify actual store listings, not promised real-world performance gains. PRODUCT-SOURCES.json in the source delivery records exact URLs. Store links open separately; game credits do not purchase physical goods.

textures/coastal-stucco.webp and textures/deco-facade.webp were generated for this game using OpenAI's built-in image generation. In-engine normal maps are derived from the base-color images. The source delivery includes prompts and paths in GENERATED-ASSETS.md.

The 20 audio/*.mp3 files were generated through ElevenLabs using the owner's supplied account authorization. These are generated interpretations, not recorded vehicle sounds. No voice was cloned. Speech uses the provider's stock George voice. The soundtrack is an original instrumental generated from a descriptive prompt without an artist reference. The account's applicable ElevenLabs commercial-use terms govern these assets; account subscription details were not accessible with the supplied key's permissions. Provider guidance: https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform
Generation prompts and original/processed file hashes are in AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json; measured loudness and peak data are in AUDIO-MIX-QA.json. No credentials or live provider connection are distributed with the game.
